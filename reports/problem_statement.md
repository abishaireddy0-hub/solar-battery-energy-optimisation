# Solar-Battery Energy Optimisation

## 1. Introduction

Commercial buildings consume significant
amounts of electrical energy.

Solar photovoltaic systems can reduce
grid electricity consumption, while battery
storage can shift energy across different
periods of the day.

However, battery storage introduces
additional investment costs.

## 2. Problem Statement

Determine the optimal battery capacity
and operating schedule for a solar-integrated
commercial building while minimising
total electricity-related costs.

## 3. Project Aim

Develop a Python-based techno-economic
optimisation framework for solar PV and
battery energy storage.

## 4. Objectives

1. Develop an hourly electricity demand model.
2. Estimate solar PV generation.
3. Model battery charging and discharging.
4. Formulate a cost-minimisation problem.
5. Evaluate different battery capacities.
6. Calculate financial performance.
7. Conduct sensitivity analysis.

## 5. Methodology

Python, Pandas, NumPy, PuLP,
linear programming and financial analysis.

## 6. Expected Outcome

An optimisation framework that identifies
an economically attractive battery capacity
and operating strategy.
